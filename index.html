Inkling Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026  ·  Android package app.inkling.draw.flutter

1. Who we are

This policy describes how the Inkling drawing and AR tracing app (“Inkling”, “we”) handles information. Contact: fatal3rrorr@gmail.com. It is written to be consistent with Türkiye’s KVKK (Law No. 6698) and the EU GDPR.

Inkling does not require an account or sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or password in order to use the app.

2. Data we process

DataPurpose
Camera imagesUsed only on your device for AR tracing (showing the paper through the camera while you trace). Camera frames are not uploaded to us or to any server.
Photos you pick from your galleryUsed only on your device as a drawing reference. They stay on the device and are not uploaded.
Image-search termsIf you search for a reference picture, the search text is sent to the Openverse API. If a Pixabay API key is configured in the app build, the same term may also be sent to Pixabay. Results are used on the device as drawing references only.
Downloaded reference imagesA selected search result may be saved temporarily on the device (in a local temp folder) so you can trace it. These files are not uploaded. Temporary references are cleared when you open the search screen again.
Your drawings, sketchbook, language and settingsStored locally on the device (the app’s documents folder and the in-app WebView’s local storage) so your progress is kept between sessions.
Purchases and subscription statusPayments are processed by Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage entitlements. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase/subscription status, and device/OS information. We do not receive your payment card details.
Drawings you choose to shareOnly if you use the share action. The file is sent solely to the app or destination you pick in the system share sheet.

Not collected: we do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not collect an advertising ID. Your data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Other on-device libraries (for example keeping the screen awake while you trace, opening these legal pages in a browser, or a “rate the app” prompt) do not send your drawings or photos to us. Opening this page or the terms page may expose your IP address to the site host (GitHub Pages), as with any website. A “rate the app” prompt may open the Google Play or App Store review interface that you confirm.

3. Third parties and international transfers

  • Openverse (WordPress.org): receives your search term and ordinary request data such as IP address when you search, and serves result thumbnails/images. Privacy: openverse.org/privacy.
  • Pixabay (optional, only when a Pixabay key is configured): same kind of search and image-fetch data. Privacy: pixabay.com/service/privacy.
  • RevenueCat: anonymous app user ID, subscription status, device and OS information. Privacy: revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store: process payments and subscriptions under their own terms and privacy policies.

These providers may process data on servers outside Türkiye. The transfer is necessary to provide search, billing and the app itself, and relies on safeguards under KVKK Art. 9 and the GDPR.

4. Retention and deletion

Camera frames are not stored by us. Local drawings, settings and temporary reference images stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall Inkling. Uninstalling the app removes the local data from that device.

Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in Google Play or App Store subscription settings. Purchase records retained by Google, Apple or RevenueCat follow those providers’ retention rules. For a deletion request about data we control, email fatal3rrorr@gmail.com.

5. Your rights (KVKK and GDPR)

Under KVKK Art. 11 and the GDPR you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restriction of or objection to processing, and (where applicable) data portability. Send requests to fatal3rrorr@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or to your local EU supervisory authority.

6. Children

Inkling is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Anyone under 18 should use the app with a parent’s or guardian’s guidance.

7. Security

Network requests (image search, purchases, opening these pages) use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Camera images, gallery photos and your drawings are kept on the device unless you choose to share a drawing.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

3. Hizmet sağlayıcılar ve yurt dışına aktarım

  • Openverse (WordPress.org): arama yaptığınızda arama teriminizi ve IP adresi gibi olağan istek verilerini alır; küçük resimleri/görselleri sunar. Gizlilik: openverse.org/privacy.

Bu sağlayıcılar verileri Türkiye dışındaki sunucularda işleyebilir. Aktarım; arama, ödeme ve uygulamanın sunulması için gereklidir ve KVKK md. 9 ile GDPR kapsamındaki güvencelere dayanır.

4. Saklama ve silme

Kamera kareleri tarafımızca saklanmaz. Yerel çizimler, ayarlar ve geçici referans görseller, uygulamada silene veya Inkling’i kaldırana kadar cihazınızda kalır. Uygulamayı kaldırmak, o cihazdaki yerel verileri siler.

Uygulamayı kaldırmak aboneliği iptal etmez. İptal için Google Play veya App Store abonelik ayarlarını kullanın. Google, Apple veya RevenueCat’in tuttuğu satın alma kayıtları bu sağlayıcıların saklama kurallarına tabidir. Kontrolümüzdeki veriler için silme talebi: fatal3rrorr@gmail.com.

KVKK md. 11 ve GDPR kapsamında kişisel verilerinize erişme, düzeltme, silme, işlemeyi kısıtlama, itiraz ve (uygulanabildiği ölçüde) taşınabilirlik haklarınız vardır. Talepleriniz için: fatal3rrorr@gmail.com. Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu’na (KVKK) veya bulunduğunuz ülkedeki AB denetim otoritesine de şikâyette bulunabilirsiniz.
